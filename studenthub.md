##### **1.Problem Statement**

##### 

StudentHub Portal



Problem:



Students have to visit different places or websites to check assignments, courses, events, notices, and their profiles. This wastes time and creates confusion.



Solution:



The StudentHub Portal provides all student-related information in one website. Students can log in, view courses, assignments, events, and manage their profiles easily.



##### **2.Scope**



The StudentHub Portal is developed for students and faculty.



The portal allows users to:



Student Registration

Student Login

View Dashboard

View Courses

View Assignments

View College Events

View Student Profile

Contact College
